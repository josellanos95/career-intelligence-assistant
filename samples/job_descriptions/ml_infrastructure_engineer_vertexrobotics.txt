Machine Learning Infrastructure Engineer
Vertex Robotics -- Onsite (Pittsburgh, PA)

About Vertex Robotics
Vertex Robotics builds computer-vision-guided robotic arms for warehouse picking. Our models run both
in the cloud for training and on embedded hardware in the field.

The Role
We need a Machine Learning Infrastructure Engineer to build and operate the training and serving
infrastructure behind our computer vision models, from raw sensor data to a deployed model on a robot.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain large-scale data ingestion pipelines (Spark, Airflow) that turn raw sensor logs into
  labeled training sets.
- Own model training infrastructure on Kubernetes, including distributed training jobs and experiment
  tracking (MLflow).
- Build model serving infrastructure (BentoML or a custom serving layer) capable of meeting strict
  latency budgets for real-time robotic control.
- Work closely with the computer vision research team to productionize new model architectures.
- Manage GPU cluster capacity and cost across training and inference workloads.

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience in ML infrastructure or MLOps, not general backend engineering.
- Deep hands-on experience with Kubernetes for both training and serving workloads.
- Experience with distributed data processing (Spark, Beam, or similar) at production scale.
- Experience with a model serving framework (BentoML, Triton, TorchServe, or similar).
- Strong Python; C++ experience is a plus for embedded inference work.
- Computer vision domain experience (image/video model training or serving).

Nice to Have
- Experience with embedded or edge model deployment (Jetson, Coral, or similar).
- Experience with experiment tracking and model registries (MLflow, SageMaker, Vertex AI).
- Prior robotics or industrial automation domain exposure.

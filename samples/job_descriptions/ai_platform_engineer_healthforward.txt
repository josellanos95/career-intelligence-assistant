AI Platform Engineer
HealthForward Analytics -- Remote (US)

About HealthForward Analytics
HealthForward Analytics builds AI-assisted tools that help clinical operations teams at mid-size hospital
networks make sense of unstructured documents -- intake forms, discharge summaries, and internal policy
PDFs. We are a 40-person Series B company selling directly to hospital IT departments.

The Role
We are hiring an AI Platform Engineer to own the retrieval and orchestration layer behind our internal
assistant product. You will work closely with a clinical operations lead who has a running list of
document types the assistant still handles poorly, and you are expected to turn that into shipped
improvements weekly, not quarterly.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain RAG pipelines (chunking, embeddings, retrieval, prompt assembly) that answer
  questions grounded strictly in uploaded hospital documents.
- Design and operate multi-step agentic workflows using LangGraph or a comparable orchestration
  framework, wiring tools such as document search, structured extraction, and human hand-off.
- Deploy and operate services in containers on Kubernetes, including basic autoscaling and rollout
  configuration.
- Add guardrails against hallucination and instrument the pipeline so a non-engineer can see why the
  assistant gave a particular answer.
- Partner with the clinical operations lead directly -- there is no product manager on this team, so you
  are expected to turn ambiguous complaints ("it missed the discharge date again") into concrete fixes.

Requirements
- 3+ years building production backend systems in Python.
- Hands-on experience with retrieval-augmented generation: chunking strategy, an embedding model, and a
  vector store, in a real shipped product (not a tutorial project).
- Experience with Docker and container orchestration (Kubernetes or ECS) in production.
- Comfortable owning a service end-to-end, including incident response when it breaks.

Nice to Have
- Experience with agent frameworks (LangGraph, AutoGen, or a custom multi-step tool-calling orchestrator).
- Prior healthcare or life-sciences domain exposure.
- Experience with structured logging, tracing, or eval harnesses for LLM-based systems.
